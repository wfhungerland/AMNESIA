# Coding rules: instrument reach (IR 1-5)

You score how far and how bindingly a measure of a leading economy (the incumbent) reaches against a rising economy (the target). Score the measure itself, not how loud the debate around it was. Work only from the sheet and, if needed, from the legal text or source named in it. Do not look at other files of the project, and do not discuss single items with the first coder.

| IR | Level | Rule | Examples |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Speech | public statement without an official procedure | a pamphlet or a speech by a minister |
| 2 | Procedure | official investigation, hearing, complaint or WTO case; nothing binding yet | a Royal Commission; a Section 301 investigation |
| 3 | Narrow binding | binding but narrow: origin marking, patent rules, an antidumping or countervailing duty on one product, obligations for single rights | a marks-of-origin act; antidumping duties on one kind of chip |
| 4 | Sectoral binding | binding quantity or price limit for a whole sector, or a trade law that creates new sanction powers | a voluntary export restraint on cars; a semiconductor agreement with price floors |
| 5 | Broad binding or denial | tariffs on a large share of imports from the target, or cutting off technology for a sector or a national champion | tariffs on hundreds of billions of imports; listing a leading firm for export licences; export controls on a whole technology |

Doubtful cases: if two levels seem possible, take the lower one and tick the "doubt" column. Use the note column for a short reason.

Procedure:
1. Read all items once before scoring.
2. Score every item with a whole number from 1 to 5.
3. Return the file under a new name, for example coding_sheet_coder2.xlsx, or the CSV version coding_sheet.csv filled in the columns IR, doubt (x if ticked) and note.

Expected time: about three and a half hours for 108 items.
